Desc: overlaps mostly static text on solid-colored backgrounds with a fashion portfolio stacked in a vertical grid 
and videos that auto-play on loading. The sticky navigation bar is undecorated and stays as plain, sans serif text 
across the top of the page. Typography is large and overlaps with photos and other visuals. Names and features are 
inconsistently capitalized.

The site’s grid layout lets users scan dozens of projects quickly without wading through paragraphs. 
This example of brutalist design puts fashion imagery first, letting the portfolio projects do the talking.
